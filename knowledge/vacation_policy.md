# Vacation and Leave Policy

## General Policy
All employees are entitled to 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year.

## Requesting Leave
1. Submit requests through the "Telvyn" portal at least 2 weeks in advance.
2. Manager approval is required for all requests over 3 consecutive days.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year. No medical certificate is required for the first 2 days of absence.
